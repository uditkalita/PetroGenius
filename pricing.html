Common Questions

Pricing FAQs

🆓 Is the trial really free?

Yes, absolutely. You get 15 full days to use PetroByte with complete features and support — no credit card, no payment required whatsoever.

💳 What payment methods are accepted?

We accept all major payment methods — UPI, Net Banking, Debit Card, Credit Card, and NEFT/RTGS transfers via our secure payment portal at pay.petrobyte.app.

❌ Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There is no lock-in. If you choose not to renew, your account moves to read-only mode. You can download all your data in Excel, CSV, or PDF at any time.

🔄 Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes. You can upgrade from Annual to Triennial or Decennial at any time. The remaining value of your current plan is adjusted against the new plan cost.

🏪 What if I have multiple petrol pumps?

Each petrol pump outlet requires a separate PetroByte licence. Contact our team for multi-outlet pricing — we offer special rates for 2+ outlets.

🛡️ Is my data safe after I stop using PetroByte?

Yes. After your plan expires, you get read-only access to all your data for up to 10 years (depending on your plan). You can always export everything.
