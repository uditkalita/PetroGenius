1. Overview

BeanByte Softwares Private Limited ("Company", "we", "us") operates PetroByte® — a cloud-based petrol pump management software. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and handle your information when you use our website (petrobyte.in) and application (petrobyte.app).

By using PetroByte, you consent to the data practices described in this Privacy Policy. This Policy is incorporated into and subject to our Terms and Conditions.

2. What Information We Collect

2.1 Account & Registration Data

  • Personal Information: Full name, mobile number, email address
  • Business Information: Petrol pump name, address, state, oil company, dealer code
  • Login Credentials: Username and encrypted password

2.2 Business & Transactional Data

When you use PetroByte to manage your petrol pump, we collect and store the business data you enter, including:

  • Daily shift data, fuel stock readings, nozzle readings
  • Financial transactions — sales, purchases, payments, receipts
  • Customer data — names, contact details, credit balances
  • Employee information — names, salary details, attendance records
  • GST and taxation information

2.3 Usage & Technical Data

  • IP address, browser type, and device information
  • Pages visited, features used, time spent on the platform
  • Error logs and performance data for debugging

2.4 Communication Data

  • Messages you send us via contact forms, email, or WhatsApp
  • Support ticket history and call records

4. Data Storage & Security

All data is stored on Google Cloud Platform servers located in India. We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest
  • OTP-based two-factor authentication for all logins
  • Daily automated backups with point-in-time recovery
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Role-based access control for our internal team
  • 99.9% uptime SLA with Google Cloud infrastructure

While we implement robust security measures, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is 100% secure. We are committed to protecting your data but cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to any third parties for marketing or commercial purposes.

We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party services that help us operate (Google Cloud for hosting, payment processors, SMS/email service providers). All are bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirement: If required by law, court order, or government authority under applicable Indian law.
  • Business Transfer: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of company assets — users will be notified in advance.
  • With Your Consent: For any other purpose with your explicit prior consent.

6. Cookies & Tracking

Our website (petrobyte.in) uses the following types of cookies:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the website to function (session management, security). Cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics Cookies: Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. Helps us improve the user experience.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences for future visits.

You can control cookie settings through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features of the website.

7. Your Rights & Data Control

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Right to Correction: Update or correct any inaccurate personal information
  • Right to Export: Download all your business data in Excel, CSV, or PDF format at any time
  • Right to Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Right to Object: Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Right to Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hi@petrobyte.in. We will respond to all requests within 30 days.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for the following periods:

  • Active Subscription: Full access to all data throughout the subscription period
  • Annual Plan (post-expiry): Read-only access for 1 year after subscription ends
  • Triennial Plan (post-expiry): Read-only access for 3 years after subscription ends
  • Decennial Plan (post-expiry): Read-only access for 10 years after subscription ends
  • After Retention Period: Data is permanently and securely deleted
  • Marketing Data: Retained until you opt out or request deletion

9. Children's Privacy

PetroByte is a business software intended for adult professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 18. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child, we will delete that information promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will notify registered users of significant changes via email or in-app notification at least 15 days before the changes take effect.

Continued use of PetroByte after any such changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
