About PetroByte

Our story — how a small team from Jaipur built India's most trusted petrol pump management software.

Our Story

Built by Developers Who Talked to Dealers

PetroByte was born out of a simple observation — petrol pump owners across India were managing crores of rupees worth of business using pen and paper or basic Excel sheets. There was no software built specifically for them.

The team at BeanByte Softwares spent months visiting petrol pumps across Rajasthan, talking to dealers, understanding their daily workflow, their pain points, and what they truly needed from a software.

The result was PetroByte — a cloud software built from the ground up, specifically for Indian petrol pump dealers, in a language and format they understand.

Our Journey

From Idea to 5000+ Customers

2019
The Idea

Team BeanByte identifies the huge gap — petrol pump owners managing ₹10L+/month operations on paper. Research and field visits begin across Rajasthan.

2020
Development Begins

Full-time development of PetroByte begins. The core shift management, accounting, and reporting modules are built and tested with beta users.

2021
First 100 Customers

PetroByte officially launches. First 100 petrol pump dealers onboard across Rajasthan and Haryana. Mobile app published on Google Play Store.

2022
TallyPrime Integration & Trademark

TallyPrime data export feature launched. PetroByte® trademark registered. Expansion to Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, and Himachal Pradesh.

2023
iOS App & GST Reports

Apple App Store app launched. Full GST (GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B) reporting module added. Customer base crosses 2,000 outlets.

2025 — Present
5000+ Customers & Growing

PetroByte now serves 5000+ petrol pumps across 15+ states. Covering BPCL, HPCL, IOCL, and Nayara Energy dealers. Mission: 50,000+ pumps by 2030.
