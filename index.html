Your Data. Your Control.

Don't let software providers lock you in. With Petro Genius, you can download, export, or transfer your data whenever you want.

Can I Access Data After Plan Expires?
Yes, Petro Genius users can access all their Financial and Managerial data of the Petrol Pump. We provide readonly access to expired users so they can easily go through their old data. They can also download data in EXCEL, CSV and PDF files.
How Long Is Data Accessible?
You can access your petrol pump business data in Petro Genius for the next 10 Years after your subscription expires. Any expired user has view-only access to all their data.
Can I Transfer Data Into Tally or Excel?
Yes, we provide MS Excel and Tally Integration feature. Download your Petro Genius data as XML files and import directly into TallyPrime, or export as Microsoft Excel spreadsheets for any use.
Is My Data Safe on the Cloud?
Absolutely. Petro Genius is hosted on Google Cloud with 99.9% uptime guarantee. All data is SSL encrypted and secured with OTP-based login. Automatic daily backups ensure your data is always protected.
